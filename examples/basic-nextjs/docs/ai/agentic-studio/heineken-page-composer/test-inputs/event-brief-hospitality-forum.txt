HEINEKEN Cambodia Hospitality Forum 2026

Building better places to meet: a half-day forum for bar, restaurant and hotel owners

Date: Thursday 3 December 2026
Time: 13:30 to 18:30
Venue: Riverside Convention Hall, Sisowath Quay, Phnom Penh
Audience: owners, managers and senior staff of bars, restaurants and hotels in Phnom Penh, Siem Reap and Battambang
Language: Khmer and English, with live interpretation
Cost: free for hospitality professionals; registration required
Registration: www.example.com/hospitality-forum-2026 (closes 26 November 2026)

About the forum

Heineken Cambodia invites the country's hospitality community to an afternoon of insights, practical workshops and networking. The forum follows the Going Out in Cambodia 2026 study, which surveyed approximately 1,500 adults in Phnom Penh, Siem Reap and Battambang. Between 64% and 81% of respondents expect bars and restaurants to remain important to social life over the next ten years, and 58% to 72% say the best venues of the future will need to offer more than drinks.

Cambodia's biggest and best hospitality event of the year, the forum brings together more than 300 venue owners and managers.
