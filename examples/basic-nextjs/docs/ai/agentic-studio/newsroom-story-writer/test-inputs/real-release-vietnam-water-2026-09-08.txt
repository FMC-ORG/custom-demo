HEINEKEN Vietnam expands water conservation programme in public-private partnership with the Ministry of Agriculture and Environment and WWF-Viet Nam, aiming for 100% water balance at all five breweries nationwide by 2030

Da Nang, 8 September 2026 – HEINEKEN Vietnam, the Ministry of Agriculture and Environment and WWF-Viet Nam today officially announced the nationwide expansion of their water conservation programme in the key river basins where HEINEKEN Vietnam's breweries operate. The event was attended by leaders of the Ministry of Agriculture and Environment, the Da Nang City People's Committee and government agencies.

This is the second phase of the project, building on the success of the public-private partnership programme launched in 2022. From 2026 to 2030, the programme will expand to the Cu De River basin (Da Nang) and the Ray River–Da Den basin (Ho Chi Minh City), while continuing water conservation in the Red River, Tien River and Dong Nai River basins.

The expansion marks an important step in HEINEKEN Vietnam's water conservation journey. Under HEINEKEN's global sustainability strategy, Brew a Better World, the company aims to achieve 100% water balance at its breweries in water-stressed areas. HEINEKEN Vietnam realised this ambition in 2025, when it balanced 100% of the water used each year at its Tien Giang brewery. Building on this, HEINEKEN Vietnam is extending its ambition to balance 100% of the water used in its products at all five of its breweries nationwide by 2030.

Viet Nam faces growing pressure on water security and increasingly visible impacts of climate change. Conserving water resources is urgent and requires collective action from the Government, local authorities, businesses, technical organisations and communities, bringing together the expertise and resources of each to create practical solutions and long-term impact.

Since 2022, HEINEKEN Vietnam has pioneered a partnership with the Ministry of Agriculture and Environment, WWF-Viet Nam and local partners to implement a water conservation programme in three river basins: the Red River, the Dong Nai River and the Tien River. By the end of 2025, the programme had replenished more than 2.2 billion litres of water a year to the three river basins and achieved 100% water balance for HEINEKEN Vietnam's three breweries in Ha Noi, Ho Chi Minh City and Tien Giang. The programme has had a positive impact, contributing to ecosystem restoration, improved water resource management, stronger community participation and better local livelihoods.

These positive results show the effectiveness of the public-private partnership model in turning sustainability commitments into concrete, long-term action. On this foundation, HEINEKEN Vietnam will expand the model to the Cu De River basin and the Ray River–Da Den basin in 2026–2030, implementing nature-based solutions and improving community access to clean water, contributing to water security and local development.

With a total investment of VND 70 billion over nine years for the whole programme from 2022 to 2030, HEINEKEN Vietnam aims to achieve 100% water balance at all five of its operating breweries by 2030. This means the company is investing in projects that restore and improve water resources in the basins where it operates, with the aim of replenishing an amount of water equivalent to the water used in the products made at its breweries.

This ambition is part of the company's commitment to contribute to Viet Nam's sustainable development, creating lasting value for the economy, society and the environment.

Speaking about the milestone, Wietse Mutters, Managing Director of HEINEKEN Vietnam, said: "Sustainability is at the heart of everything we do. For 35 years, we have grown alongside Viet Nam, working with our partners to pioneer and innovate in addressing the challenges we face together. Since 2022, we have partnered with the Ministry of Agriculture and Environment, WWF-Viet Nam and local communities to bring together different expertise and resources to protect water resources in some of Viet Nam's key river basins. The progress we have made gives us confidence to scale up this partnership further, as we work towards our ambition of achieving 100 per cent water balance across all our breweries by 2030, and continuing to create positive impact for Viet Nam."

Speaking at the event, Nguyen Do Anh Tuan, Director General of the Department of International Cooperation, Ministry of Agriculture and Environment, said: "The public-private partnership between HEINEKEN Vietnam, WWF-Viet Nam and the Ministry of Agriculture and Environment has demonstrated what can be achieved when different stakeholders come together around a shared goal. Water security is becoming an increasingly important issue for Viet Nam, particularly as we enter a new era where growth goes hand in hand with sustainable development. The contributions of businesses such as HEINEKEN Vietnam are highly valuable in protecting and preserving our water resources."

Over 35 years in Viet Nam, HEINEKEN Vietnam has pursued its commitment "Brewing a Better Vietnam" and a sustainability strategy built on three pillars: Responsible Consumption, Environment and Social, pioneering effective public-private partnerships.

As one of the foreign-invested enterprises making high-value contributions to Viet Nam, in 2025 alone HEINEKEN Vietnam contributed the equivalent of 0.5% of national GDP to the economy and created 167,000 jobs across its value chain. The company prioritises local sourcing and spent nearly VND 22 trillion on it, creating value for communities and for domestic value and supply chains in Viet Nam.

The company is committed to continuing to support Viet Nam's growth and sustainable development through long-term investments that create positive impact for the economy, society and the environment.
