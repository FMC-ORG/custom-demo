**Record premium growth and margin expansion as EverGreen 2030 delivers ahead of plan**

## Growth: Premium and LoNo leading the portfolio

- Total volume increased 2.4% in the third quarter; consolidated volume grew 1.1% and licensed volume up 19.8%.
- Heineken® volume up 6.1%, with double-digit growth in Brazil, Vietnam and South Africa.
- Premium grew 8%, beyond beer up 10%, and low- and no-alcohol 15% higher.
- Net revenue growth of 4.1%, with growth in all four regions.

## Profitability: Margin expansion continues

- Operating profit grew 7.9% in the first nine months; operating profit margin expanded 70 bps to 15.3%.
- Diluted Earnings per Share (EPS) of €3.61 for the first nine months, up 12.4% (2025: €3.21).
- Gross savings of €410 million year to date, on track to exceed the €400–500 million annual target.

## Capital Efficiency: Strong cash delivery

- Free Operating Cash Flow of €3.2 billion in the first nine months, a cash conversion ratio of 102%.
- Second tranche of the €1.5 billion share buyback programme completed.
- Net debt to EBITDA improved to 2.1x.

## EverGreen 2030: Ahead of plan

- 60+ innovations launched, contributing 7% of net revenue.
- Digital B2B platforms now serve 750,000 customers in 30 markets.
- Renewable energy reached 78% of thermal energy across production sites.
- FY2026 operating profit growth guidance raised to 4% to 8% (previously 2% to 6%).

## CFO statement

"The third quarter of 2026 showed disciplined execution. We grew volume ahead of our markets, expanded margins again and delivered our productivity programme earlier than planned. Our premium portfolio and our low- and no-alcohol brands are now growing at twice the pace of the total business. We enter the final quarter with momentum, a stronger balance sheet and a clear focus on the consumers and customers who make HEINEKEN the world's most international brewer."

## Outlook 2026

Based on the performance in the first nine months, we expect a gradually improving consumer environment in most of our markets and remain confident in the continued execution of EverGreen 2030. For the full year 2026 we now anticipate:

- Operating profit to grow between 4% and 8% (previously 2% to 6%).
- Gross savings of around €500 million, offsetting most of the expected input cost inflation.
- An average effective interest rate of around 3.4%.
- An effective tax rate (ETR) of around 27.5%.
- Capital expenditure below 7.5% of net revenue.
